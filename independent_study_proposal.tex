% 1. Overview
% ------------------------------------------------------------
\section{Overview}

\textbf{physio-placebo: Is the LLM Reading the Signal, or Is the Verbalizer
Doing the Classifying?}

It is a surrogate-controlled audit of the fast-growing ``LLMs reason over your
physiology'' literature: take published prompting pipelines (Health-LLM style),
run them under strict leave-one-subject-out on WESAD / MAUS / CogWear, then
systematically replace the physiological signal with spectrum-matched noise,
a wrong subject's recording, a flatline, or nothing at all, and measure how
much of the above-chance margin survives. The sharp part is the second move:
separating \textbf{verbalizer leakage} (the template says ``elevated skin
conductance, stress-typical'') from actual signal information, which nobody
has done.

% ------------------------------------------------------------
% 2. The falsifiable core question
% ------------------------------------------------------------
\section{The falsifiable core question}

\begin{quote}
\textbf{Does an LLM's above-chance performance on physiological state
classification survive replacing the signal with spectrum-matched noise,
another subject's recording, or nothing at all, and does the
feature-verbalized condition keep its advantage once class-suggestive
adjectives are stripped from the template --- or is the measured performance
produced by the prompt scaffolding and the label prior rather than by the
physiology?}
\end{quote}

\section{Concrete semester plan (15 weeks)}

\textbf{Compute reality check:} every condition is scored by reading
\textbf{logprobs over the label tokens in a single forward pass}, not free
generation. At 8B in 4-bit with vLLM batching, the full grid is roughly
20--40 GPU-hours. A 16GB card is sufficient.

\textbf{Datasets (all downloadable week 1, no gatekeeper):} WESAD (UCI, 15
subjects, ECG\slash EDA\slash BVP\slash RESP, binary stress vs non-stress as
the standard task),
MAUS (IEEE DataPort, 22 subjects, wrist PPG under n-back), CogWear (PhysioNet,
11 subjects, Empatica E4 + Muse S, Stroop vs baseline). Stretch: DD-Database
(Dryad), OpenNeuro ds003838.

\textbf{Models:} Qwen3-8B-Instruct and Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct (4-bit);
Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct for the plot paradigm; optional GPT-class API as a paid
upper bound ($<$\$50).

% ------------------------------------------------------------
% 5. What could kill it, and the fix
% ------------------------------------------------------------
\section{What could kill it, and the fix}

\textbf{Objection 1: ``Your surrogates are not nulls, so your audit is
measuring nothing.''}\\
Moment-matched Gaussian noise and IAAFT phase-randomization both
\textbf{preserve per-channel mean and variance} --- and mean HR and tonic EDA
level are exactly where most of the class information in this data lives. A
surrogate that preserves those is leaking the label, which \emph{deflates} SDS
and makes a working model look like a placebo. The label-preserving subject
permutation as originally specified has the opposite bug: wrong person but
right condition is a real, correctly-labelled physiological signal, so high
performance there is not evidence of cheating at all.\\
\textbf{Fix, built into the design:} (a) moments are matched to the
\textbf{globally pooled} distribution, never per-class; (b) subject
permutation draws a wrong subject \textbf{and} an independently sampled
condition, so label alignment is destroyed; (c) the label-preserving
permutation is retained as a \emph{separate, differently-named} condition
(``population-level vs individual-level information''), reported but never
counted as a null; (d) the \textbf{information-preservation ledger} runs the
classical LR on every surrogate and publishes exactly how much class
information each one retains, so no surrogate's status is asserted, it is
measured.

\textbf{Objection 2: ``You built it wrong / your SDS is unstable, so the
negative result is your bug.''}\\
Two failure modes. First, prompt quality: any null can be dismissed as bad
prompting. Second and more serious, \textbf{SDS
$= (M_{\mathrm{real}} - M_{\mathrm{surrogate}}) \, / \,
(M_{\mathrm{real}} - M_{\mathrm{chance}})$ explodes or inverts when
$M_{\mathrm{real}}$ is near chance} --- precisely the case for the
raw-numeric-series paradigm, where the denominator is close to zero and the
ratio becomes meaningless noise.\\
\textbf{Fix:} (a) the week-3 reproduce-before-audit fidelity anchor against
released code, with the reproduction gap reported honestly; (b) each condition
is evaluated at \textbf{its own best prompt}, selected on a held-out fold and
frozen before surrogates run; (c) a pre-registered \textbf{SDS validity gate}
--- if a condition's real-signal macro-F1 does not exceed the
label-permutation chance floor by a margin whose bootstrap CI excludes zero,
SDS is declared undefined for that cell and only the raw delta with CI is
reported. No ratio is ever printed on top of a near-zero denominator;
(d) subject is the unit of resampling throughout, so the n=11--22 subject
counts are honestly reflected in every interval.

\emph{Secondary risk: ``everyone already knew LLMs
can't read raw PPG.'' The defence is that the published literature claims
otherwise and reports gains, and the verbalizer arm has a genuinely
non-obvious answer either way. If results come out positive, this becomes the
first evidence that LLM physiological reasoning survives surrogate controls
--- still publishable, which is why week-3 pre-registration matters.}

% 6. The project in one sentence

\section{The project in one sentence}

\begin{quote}
I'd like to test whether the LLMs that claim to reason over physiology are
actually using the signal: take WESAD and MAUS, run the published prompting
pipelines, then swap the real recording for phase-randomized noise or another
subject's data and see how much accuracy survives, because if it survives then
the LLM layer people are bolting onto sensors like ours is mostly reading the
prompt template.
\end{quote}

\textbf{More technically:}
\begin{quote}
I want to build a surrogate-control harness for LLM physiological inference:
IAAFT phase-randomization, subject permutation and signal deletion across
WESAD, MAUS and CogWear under leave-one-subject-out, with a logistic
regression on the same HRV and EDA features as the floor, so we can report how
much of a paper's claimed margin is signal and how much is the verbalizer's
adjectives.
\end{quote}

\textbf{The underlying curiosity:}
\begin{quote}
I keep noticing that every ``LLM reads your biosignals'' paper turns the
features into sentences like ``elevated skin conductance, stress-typical''
before the model ever sees them, and I really want to find out whether the
model is classifying the person or just reading that adjective back to us.
\end{quote}

\section{A second idea, in brief}

\textbf{A gaze-grounded reward-model audit:} does the reward-model margin
favour responses that cost more to read, after residualizing out length, word
frequency, word length and surprisal, using OASST-ETC's real fixation data
scored by Skywork-Reward-V2-8B, ArmoRM and a Qwen3-8B judge. Week 1 must be a
hard go/no-go on actually obtaining the OASST-ETC fixation files, since no
substitute corpus exists.
