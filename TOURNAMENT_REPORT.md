**Red-team: 6/10** — Execution risk is near zero and the harness is a real artifact, but the science has two concrete defects and a boringness problem. (1) THE HEADLINE METRIC IS DEGENERATE EXACTLY WHERE THE HYPOTHESIS IS TRUE. SDS = (M_real - M_surrogate)/(M_real - M_chance). The stated prediction is that raw-series and plot paradigms fail; if they fail, M_real is at chance, the denominator goes to zero, and SDS explodes or is undefined with unbounded bootstrap CIs. You cannot audit a capability that does not exist. The EEG-to-text audit worked because the target papers reported implausibly high BLEU produced by a specific artifact (teacher forcing) that could be named and removed; here there is no comparably concrete artifact to expose in the raw-series arm, just weak performance. Fix: report absolute macro-F1 with CIs as the primary table and demote SDS to a secondary ratio computed only where M_real is significantly above chance. (2) SURROGATE #3 IS NOT A NULL. 'Subject-permuted, real physiology, wrong person, identical label distribution' preserves the label if you swap subject A's stress segment for subject B's stress segment. WESAD's TSST response is highly stereotyped across people, so the permuted surrogate carries genuine class information; performance will stay high and the authors will read SDS ~ 0 as 'no signal dependence' when it actually means 'the surrogate was never a null.' This is a design bug, not a risk. Fix: permute across labels or across protocol timepoints, and report a shuffled-label ceiling. (3) CEILING/FLOOR SQUEEZE: WESAD stress-vs-baseline is near-solved by tonic EDA mean alone; the depth-3 tree baseline will be at ~90%+, so any LLM condition that touches a verbalized EDA feature inherits the answer, and the interesting contrast compresses. (4) THE VERBALIZER ABLATION MAY BE DEFINITIONALLY CONFUSED. Replacing 'elevated skin conductance' with a raw number plus a population percentile does not remove class information, it re-encodes it; the percentile IS the discriminative feature. So 'the verbalizer is doing the classification' reduces to 'features do the classification and the LLM is a wrapper,' which is a tautology of any feature pipeline rather than a finding. (5) TRUE-BUT-BORING: unlike EEG-to-text, the community prior here is already skeptical; confirming that an 8B model cannot read raw PPG from a prompt surprises nobody. (6) LOSO on n=15 (WESAD) / n=11 (CogWear) gives per-subject F1 spreads wide enough that most of the 3x2x2x5 cells will have overlapping CIs.

> Killer objection: The subject-permuted surrogate is label-preserving in protocol-rigid datasets like WESAD, so a headline 'the LLM is not signal-dependent' result can be manufactured by a broken control rather than by an actual shortcut.

### FINALIST 1 — 26/40

That is falsifiable, novel, and publishable in either direction — the rare finalist where the null is a real result rather than a shrug. Arm B is weaker on novelty but strong on execution: the critique itself is already in print (the ETRA 2026 paper 'Gaze patterns predict preference and confidence in pairwise AI image evaluation', arXiv 2603.24849, states outright that existing gaze-RM work uses synthetic gaze that 'does not capture actual human attention', and OASST-ETC itself already compares real vs synthetic reading patterns), so the *observation* is not the contribution — the surrogate-controlled audit (phase-shuffled, subject-permuted, time-shuffled, length-only) is, and importing the Jo et al. Secondary: n=24 participants on one corpus means Arm A's headline is a single-dataset correlational result, and a hostile reviewer who does not buy the residualization (length dominates every eye-tracking measure) can reject on confounding alone, no matter how many control specs are reported.

**Red-team: 5/10** — I checked the corpus and it is much smaller than the plan implies. OASST-ETC: 360 prompts, 720 responses, 24 participants, each participant evaluating 30 prompts x 2 responses = ~60 readings. That is ~720 pair-readings over 360 unique items, i.e. roughly TWO gaze records per item. Three attacks follow. (1) ARM B IS DEAD ON POWER, NOT SALVAGEABLE. Training a Bradley-Terry head with LoRA on <=360 unique pairs, with participant-disjoint splits, across SIX conditions, and asking whether (b) real gaze beats (d) length-and-frequency surrogate gaze: the held-out set is on the order of 70-120 pairs, so the accuracy CI is roughly +/-9-11 points, while the effect being chased (GazeReward-style gains) is a few points. Every condition will overlap every other condition. The TOST equivalence tests will return bounds so wide they are compatible with both 'gaze helps a lot' and 'gaze does nothing,' which is the opposite of an interpretable null. The plan's own week-1 power calculation, run honestly, should cancel Arm B. (2) THE ARM A HEADLINE REQUIRES A COVARIATE-INDUCED SIGN FLIP. The corpus paper's own result is that REJECTED responses had significantly higher total reading time, first fixation duration, and fixation counts, while CHOSEN responses were LONGER and had longer words. So at the raw level, preferred text is read with LESS total effort, which is the opposite direction from 'reward models reward surplus reading cost.' The hypothesis can only come out positive after residualizing length out, i.e. the entire finding depends on the sign flipping under covariate adjustment. Reviewers will correctly read that as a suppressor/Simpson's artifact of a collinear covariate set (token count, word length, mean log frequency, and surprisal are mutually collinear, and the residual is dominated by misspecification plus rendering effects like code blocks and line wrapping, not by 'surplus difficulty'). The three-spec sign-stability requirement does not fix this; it just tests whether the artifact is stable. (3) CONSTRUCT VALIDITY: participants were reading in order to JUDGE. Re-reading and regressions in a forced-choice comparison measure comparison strategy and pair similarity, not intrinsic processing cost. 'Reading effort' as an ecological cost-of-use proxy is not what this task elicits. (4) Same surrogate bug as Finalist 2: 'subject-permuted real gaze' swaps subject A's gaze on item X for subject B's gaze on item X, which preserves the item-level signal entirely and is therefore not a null. (5) The prompt random intercept is barely identified at ~2 observations per prompt. What survives is a modest descriptive table with attenuated, probably null correlations and an honest 'measurement noise dominates' conclusion.

> Killer objection: The corpus is ~360 items with ~2 gaze records each, which kills Arm B on power outright, and the corpus's own published raw effect runs opposite to the Arm A hypothesis, so a positive result exists only as a sign flip produced by residualizing collinear covariates.

K-EmoCon self annotations were produced by participants ANNOTATING THE DEBATE FOOTAGE afterwards on the same 5-second grid as the partner and external raters. The 'self' target is therefore a retrospective third-person judgment of one's own video with privileged memory, not the felt state. H1 as written ('LLMs align with observers rather than with what the person actually felt') is testing a contrast the data cannot instantiate; the honest version is 'LLM agrees more with 5 averaged strangers than with 1 self-rater,' which is substantially explained by (2) below and is close to true-but-trivial. (2) FLOOR EFFECT FROM RATER NOISE PLUS THE AVERAGING ASYMMETRY. K-EmoCon's cross-perspective agreement is notoriously weak. The external target is the MEAN of 5 raters and is therefore variance-reduced; the self target is a single rater. CCC against a 5-rater mean will beat CCC against a 1-rater signal for purely psychometric reasons, independent of any perspective effect. That is a trivial baseline that reproduces the headline with no LLM insight at all, and the plan has no reliability correction (disattenuation, or subsampling external raters to n=1) to defend against it. (3) RESTRICTED RANGE: continuous arousal/valence in naturalistic debate is heavily concentrated near neutral, which crushes CCC toward zero for every target and makes the between-target difference a comparison of two near-zero numbers. (4) H2's CONFOUND IS MOVEMENT, NOT SPEECH RATE. Wrist Empatica E4 EDA during an animated seated debate is dominated by gesticulation and pressure artifacts. Gesticulation also drives observer-perceived arousal and the LLM-self residual via transcript disfluency. The stated text baseline (NRC-VAD, word count, speech rate, pronouns, turn position) does not control motion; you would need accelerometer-based artifact regression from the E4, which is not in the plan. (5) UNIT OF ANALYSIS: 32 participants but 16 dyadic debates, and within a dyad the two participants' states, transcripts, and physiology are strongly coupled. The effective n for anything conversation-level is 16, not 32, and participant-only random intercepts understate the SEs. (6) A 15% CCC reduction in H3 on effectively 16 conversations with a near-neutral rating distribution is not a powered target. (7) Access gate: Zenodo restricted request with unbounded latency, and the stated fallback (OASST-ETC) inherits all of Finalist 1's small-n problems. The subject-permuted control in H3 is the one genuinely excellent piece of design here.

> Killer objection: K-EmoCon 'self' ratings are retrospective video annotations, not felt experience, and the external target is a 5-rater mean versus a 1-rater self target, so the headline result is predicted by rater-averaging psychometrics alone and the interoception-versus-behaviour construct the whole project rests on is not measurable in this corpus.

**Red-team: 3/10** — Highest-risk finalist and also the one whose Phase 1 'contract' is the most circular. (1) PHASE 1 IS A LEAKAGE MACHINE. The instrument (mean surprisal of the user's turn given the dialogue prefix; semantic drift between consecutive user turns) and the label (auto-detected rephrase / clarification-request per the published taxonomy) are BOTH monotone functions of lexical and semantic overlap with the preceding context. Features (a), (c) and (d) are three measurements of one construct. A trivial baseline (token-level Jaccard overlap between user turn t and turn t-1) will match or beat the whole 20-sample dispersion pipeline at a thousandth of the compute, which also makes the LLM component decorative. 'Is reward score at turn t associated with struggle at t+1' over WildChat: hard/ambiguous requests produce both lower-quality answers AND more follow-ups. Item difficulty is the confound and there is no instrument for it. (3) THE THEORY DOES NOT PREDICT A SIGN. A cognitively overloaded user often becomes MORE predictable ('I still don't get it', 'can you explain again'), not less; a fluent expert exploring freely is highly unpredictable. Prompt-predictive dispersion is at least as plausibly an engagement/expertise measure as a load measure, so either sign confirms the story, which is the definition of an unfalsifiable construct. Visual detection-probe RT during a reading/typing task is dominated by whether the user is mid-keystroke, mid-read, or idle, and reading LLM output visually occludes the probe. That is a phase confound, not workload, and it correlates with turn boundaries, which is exactly where the surprisal features are computed. (5) H3 IS PROBABLY TOPIC CLASSIFICATION. The difficulty manipulation changes the problem content, so LOSO AUC > 0.65 for high- vs low-difficulty blocks from LLM-derived text features is trivially achievable by detecting which problem set the user is on. It is not evidence of load sensing at all, and the plan has no content-matched control (e.g. same problem domain at two difficulty levels with matched vocabulary). (6) H2 is pre-committed honest and pre-committed likely-false: pause duration and revision bursts are established, strong load indices, and the LLM features are unlikely to add incremental variance over them. (7) n=20-25 for a within-subject turn-level effect nested under ~60 probes each, behind an Indian IEC gate the plan itself flags as the binding constraint. If ethics slips, what remains is Phase 1, which is the circular part.

> Killer objection: The Phase 1 instrument and the Phase 1 labels are both functions of overlap between consecutive user turns, so the standalone deliverable is guaranteed to 'work' for a purely definitional reason and is beaten by a one-line lexical-overlap baseline, while the load construct itself has no theoretically determined sign.

## 5. What could kill it, and the fix

**Objection 1: "Your surrogates are not nulls, so your audit is measuring nothing."**
Moment-matched Gaussian noise and IAAFT phase-randomization both **preserve per-channel mean and variance** — and mean HR and tonic EDA level are exactly where most of the class information in this data lives. A surrogate that preserves those is leaking the label, which *deflates* SDS and makes a working model look like a placebo. The label-preserving subject permutation as originally specified has the opposite bug: wrong person but right condition is a real, correctly-labelled physiological signal, so high performance there is not evidence of cheating at all.
**Fix, built into the design:** (a) moments are matched to the **globally pooled** distribution, never per-class; (b) subject permutation draws a wrong subject **and** an independently sampled condition, so label alignment is destroyed; (c) the label-preserving permutation is retained as a *separate, differently-named* condition ("population-level vs individual-level information"), reported but never counted as a null; (d) the **information-preservation ledger** runs the classical LR on every surrogate and publishes exactly how much class information each one retains, so no surrogate's status is asserted, it is measured.

**Objection 2: "You built it wrong / your SDS is unstable, so the negative result is your bug."**
Two failure modes. First, prompt quality: any null can be dismissed as bad prompting. Second and more serious, **SDS = (M_real − M_surrogate) / (M_real − M_chance) explodes or inverts when M_real is near chance** — precisely the case for the raw-numeric-series paradigm, where the denominator is close to zero and the ratio becomes meaningless noise.
**Fix:** (a) the week-3 reproduce-before-audit fidelity anchor against released code, with the reproduction gap reported honestly; (b) each condition is evaluated at **its own best prompt**, selected on a held-out fold and frozen before surrogates run; (c) a pre-registered **SDS validity gate** — if a condition's real-signal macro-F1 does not exceed the label-permutation chance floor by a margin whose bootstrap CI excludes zero, SDS is declared undefined for that cell and only the raw delta with CI is reported. No ratio is ever printed on top of a near-zero denominator; (d) subject is the unit of resampling throughout, so the n=11–22 subject counts are honestly reflected in every interval.

*Secondary risk worth naming to the prof: "everyone already knew LLMs can't read raw PPG." The defence is that the published literature claims otherwise and reports gains, and the verbalizer arm has a genuinely non-obvious answer either way. If results come out positive, this becomes the first evidence that LLM physiological reasoning survives surrogate controls — still publishable, which is why week-3 pre-registration matters.*
